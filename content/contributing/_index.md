This is a grass-roots, community project. Anyone is welcome to make contributions, and it's more important for us to create an inclusive and welcoming experience for contributors than to work as efficiently as possible.
{{</hero>}}

## How we treat each other

We're building a better future together, and _**how**_ we do that matters. For the time being we're using the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/) as our Code of Conduct. Reporting issues in your experience of working on this project is an important part of repairing harm and reducing it for others in the future. If you have any concerns, please [contact us](/contact) either with your issue, or to be put in touch with whichever committee member you would feel most safe to discuss your concerns with.
