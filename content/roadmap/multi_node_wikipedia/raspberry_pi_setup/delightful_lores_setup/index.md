## Solution

While setting the admin password is important, the user may have just learned a bunch of new concepts, like co-op cloud and docker, and deserves a moment of **celebration**.

More than that, we want to **inspire** them. There's a local network they can build, to help their neighbours prepare for the climate crisis, and they get to help shape what it looks like.

This work needs a properly considered **User Experience** design. It could developed as wire-frames, and then handed over to developers.
